Rubric revision and interpretation
The expanded task permits genuine argumentative relevance without requiring logical entailment of a policy choice. This corrects the earlier narrow primary party/religion framing. The positive position is explicit per record. Supports/challenges describe relevance to that policy, never a group referent by itself. Direct marks a stated argument, concrete benefit/cost, or relevant causal mechanism. Interpretation_dependent marks a plausible argument requiring a stated value, comparison, or institutional frame. It is not a separate direction. Unknown marks missing key, wording, axis, or contextual evidence; ambiguous marks competing directions. Neutral includes recognition, officeholding, and stance-location facts with no independent policy-merit content.

Do not mechanically use every signed row in estimation. All rows set analysis_ready=false because this is an independent screen preceding adjudication. Some signed rows have no verified baseline field, and those are explicit research leads, not immediately linkable observations. candidate_baseline_field is non-null only when actual variable existence and source wording/labels were checked; New Haven's number-to-column match is separately flagged provisional because metadata labels are blank. Value labels are retained where available. Missing baseline fields are not proof that the study lacks an attitude, and unknown is not exclusion of the entire poll.

Other leads and limitations
AMR has development, stewardship, and infection-prevention candidates, but exact matching baseline policy fields remain to be located. Its combined final item contains both access and misuse harms and is ambiguous. BTP 2007 voting restrictions/gerrymandering, UK health needs, UK EU tax autonomy, Europolis wind/overstay enforcement, Tomorrow's Europe cooperation/aid, and Marousi Metro service are further candidates, not silently discarded for lack of generic identity. Many named-party position questions are useful mapping evidence but not automatically congenial facts. Historical economic performance facts need an actual incumbent-evaluation axis and must not be represented as causal policy effects.

Every row has axis_direction_coverage. Single-direction batteries can identify differences in gains between respondents whose baseline positions make those facts congenial or uncongenial. They cannot identify a same-person contrast across both directions on the same policy axis. Do not balance batteries by inventing signs or by promoting unresolved keys. Different axes in the same poll are not substitutes for within-axis opposite directions.
