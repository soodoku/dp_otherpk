Expanded independent screen A — 10 October 2026

Coverage: all 260 items in metadata/item_inventory.json across 32 polls. This is provisional model-assisted screening, not adjudication, human validation or blinded validation. No other coder output or new generated analyses were inspected.

Exposure: the prior pilot accidentally exposed aggregate Northern Ireland report results. In this expanded pass, AMR harmonized-codebook.pdf pages 32–34 unexpectedly displayed pooled response summaries, and keyword searches of CPL/WTU/SWEPCO codebook.txt returned embedded pre/post frequencies. These source-codebook statistics were not used in any direction assignment. The expanded pass did not inspect empirical papers or new report results. After detecting codebook statistics, extraction was restricted to metadata/question wording. General model training exposure cannot be known.

Coding rule: a correct proposition may supply a benefit, harm, constraint, mechanism, feasibility argument or performance signal without proving the entire policy choice. Concrete direct arguments are clear. Endorsements, isolated magnitudes, credentials, partial comparisons and competing interpretations remain interpretation-dependent. Neutral, ambiguous and unknown are retained with reasons. These labels are not derived from respondent answers or learning gaps.
